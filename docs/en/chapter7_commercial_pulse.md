## 7.1 From "Transit Through-Node" to "TOD Commercial Living Circle"

In classical transportation engineering, stations were evaluated narrowly as "Transit Nodes," with performance gauged by dwell times and evacuation clearance seconds.  
Under modern Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) frameworks, stations have transformed into **"Urban Destinations"** and **"Civic Anchors"**:

* **Taipei Main Station** is not merely a tri-modal rail interchange; it is a mega-commercial complex integrating the Breeze Food Plaza, Q Square Mall, and underground shopping streets.
* **Banqiao Station** harnesses Christmasland crowds to generate enormous retail revenues for the surrounding Global Mall and Mega City department stores.
* **THSR Taichung and THSR Tainan Stations** are expanding into massive entertainment shopping complexes and Mitsui Outlet lifestyle centers.

To quantify how transit flows convert into brick-and-mortar economic vitality, this system presents the **Commercial Pulse Lab**, evaluating metropolitan districts alongside **30 strategic transit hubs** across Taiwan.

### Mode A: Urban District Commercial Dynamic Index
Evaluates core metropolitan districts across northern, central, and southern Taiwan (e.g., Daan and Xinyi in Taipei; Xitun in Taichung; Zuoying in Kaohsiung):

* **Footfall Activity**: Dynamic volume of daytime workers and evening residential populations.
* **Spending Propensity**: Integrates transit alighting flows with commercial grading to estimate marginal consumption potential.
* **Green Transit Penetration**: Proportion of residents utilizing rail, bus, and cycling for daily mobility.

### Mode B: Deep Dives into Taiwan's Top 30 Strategic Transit Hubs
Focuses on 30 of Taiwan's highest-traffic hubs—including **Taipei, Banqiao, Nangang, THSR Hsinchu, THSR Taichung, Tainan, Zuoying, Taoyuan, Zhongli, Hualien, and Yilan**—establishing centimeter-accurate catchment profiles.

## 7.3 Dual Investment Engines: Retail Driving Rays & EV Charging ROI

The Commercial Lab incorporates two proprietary decision models to bridge the gap between rail transit and private automotive catchment zones:

### 1. 🏢 Scheme A: Flagship Retail Driving Catchment Rays
Deconstructs cross-county driving catchments for 12 mega shopping malls, outlet centers, and Costco wholesale warehouses:

* **Cross-County Catchment Rays (OD Catchment Rays)**: By tracing eTag interchange flows, the system maps the origin living spheres and highway corridors used by shoppers (e.g., Mitsui Outlet Park Linkou draws 44.5% of visitors from Taipei, 32% from Taoyuan, and 14.5% from Hsinchu).
* **Average Basket Value & Consumer Demographics**: Pairs vehicle flow attributes with household income profiles to estimate mean transaction sizes (NT$ 5,680) and family retail preferences.

### 2. ⚡ Scheme B: Freeway EV High-Speed Supercharging Site Selection & CPO ROI Engine
Synthesizes daily gantry traffic across 325 freeway points with intercity travel mileage to evaluate station utilization and dynamic payback periods for 360kW liquid-cooled EV superchargers across 10 strategic nodes:

* **Dynamic ROI Simulation Engine**: Users customize the number of stalls (e.g., 4 dispensers, 8 plugs) and retail electricity tariff (NT$ 9.8~11.8 / kWh); the engine instantly computes equipment utilization, annual revenue, and dynamic payback periods.
* **Taipower Feeder Capacity Assessment**: Evaluates 11.4kV extra-high-voltage feeder capacity and grid connection complexity, recommending premier Charge Point Operators (Tesla Supercharger V4, U-POWER, Fortune Electric).

### 3. 🍔 F&B Grab-and-Go Meal Time Slots (F&B Radar)
Analyzes passenger turnstile tap distributions to deconstruct the prime operating windows for takeaway food and beverage retail:

* **Breakfast Grab-and-Go Rush (07:15 - 08:45)**: Alighting passengers dwell for under 3 minutes; revenue is driven by pre-packaged coffee, sandwiches, and rice balls aligned along primary passenger circulation paths.
* **Business Lunch Window (11:30 - 13:30)**: Driven by dine-in seating, premium bento boxes, and casual business dining.
* **Dinner & Evening Indulgence (17:30 - 20:30)**: Takeaway hot meals, souvenir confectioneries, and family restaurant dining before returning home.

## 7.6 🔍 Core Commercial Takeaways

> **💡 Real Estate Investment Takeaway**:  
> Footfall volume does not equal purchasing power. Commuters moving at 1.5 m/s during the morning rush represent zero dine-in opportunity, but exceptional pre-packaged grab-and-go revenue. Conversely, travelers arriving during evening or weekend intervals possess high dwell times and large basket sizes, making experiential dining and retail pop-ups extraordinarily lucrative.
